Massachusetts Clean Energy Center

About the program

The Massachusetts Clean Energy Center funds an offshore wind educational grant administered through the Gloucester Fishermen's Wives Development Project. The grant pays for the safety and survival training that commercial fishermen and other mariners need in order to qualify for work in the offshore wind industry — training that otherwise comes out of pocket.

I coordinate the program: application intake, eligibility review, funding approval, and outreach to mariners, training schools, and community organizations.
