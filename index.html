Grant Coordination

Funding the move from fishing to offshore wind

Since December 2025 I have been project coordinator for the Gloucester Fishermen's Wives Development Project, managing Massachusetts Clean Energy Center grant funds that support mariner communities across the Commonwealth. The grant covers the cost of the safety and survival training that commercial fishermen need to qualify for offshore wind work — training that otherwise runs hundreds of dollars out of pocket.

  • Application intake, eligibility review, and funding approval
  • Outreach to fishermen, training schools, and community organizations
  • Documentation, reporting, and outcome tracking

Need mariner training funded?

If you work on the water in Massachusetts, you may qualify to have your certification paid for. Applications are open.
